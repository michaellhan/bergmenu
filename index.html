Add to Home Screen

Open the menu like an app, full screen, with one tap.

iPhone & iPad (Safari)

  1. Tap the Share button (in newer Safari, tap ··· first).
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top corner.
  2. Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app).
  3. Tap Add or Install.
